Who votes—and who might turn out?

Age, education, and place · historical-style benchmarks and a 2026 scenario

Illustrative midterm turnout rates by age, educational attainment, and community type.

Midterm participation is uneven. Age, education, and where a person lives are associated with different turnout rates, but the differences are not fixed from one election to the next. The chart combines rounded, illustrative benchmark estimates for 2018 and 2022 with a modeled 2026 scenario. None should be read as certified official demographic counts.

Participation by group

The bars compare each group’s estimated turnout in the three midterm cycles. The 2026 values are scenario projections, not exit polls or post-election estimates. Group categories are broad and can conceal large differences within each group.

Important

Projection ≠ result. The 2026 bars show a scenario assumption only. Turnout estimates depend on the population denominator, eligibility rules, survey design, and whether rates are measured among citizens, voting-age residents, or eligible voters.

What the comparison can—and cannot—show

  • Age: Older voters tend to participate at higher rates in midterms, so shifts in the age mix of the electorate can change the overall result even when group-level preferences stay constant.
  • Education: Educational attainment is associated with participation, but it is also correlated with age, income, geography, and other factors. These bars do not isolate a causal effect.
  • Community type: Urban, suburban, and rural categories vary by definition and state. A national-style comparison can hide important local patterns.
  • 2026: Treat the final column as a transparent placeholder for a forecast. Replace it with a named, dated, documented source before drawing conclusions about the actual electorate.

The demographic values are deliberately rounded, synthetic examples. They are not sourced estimates, survey results, Census counts, or certified 2026 turnout. The chart demonstrates a comparison workflow and should be updated with documented data before publication as factual analysis.
