Battlegrounds: the close contests

Eight state-level snapshots · scenario data, not live polling

Illustrative Senate and House battleground margins and campaign finance comparisons.

The map is a mix of statewide Senate contests and competitive House districts. The figures below are synthetic scenario inputs designed to show how to compare races: they are not actual polls, campaign disclosures, or official Cook Political Report ratings. “Rating” is a Cook-style label for this demonstration only.

Margin and uncertainty

The dot is the scenario’s Democratic two-party margin; positive values favor the Democratic candidate. The horizontal interval shows an assumed ±4-point uncertainty band, not a poll’s published margin of error.

Fundraising and modeled vote share

Each point is a race. The horizontal axis combines both candidates’ scenario fundraising totals; the vertical axis translates the assumed margin into a Democratic two-party vote share. A larger combined fundraising total does not imply a closer race or a causal effect on vote share.

All margins, uncertainty bands, fundraising totals, modeled shares, and ratings in these charts are fabricated for demonstration. State/race labels identify plausible contest types; candidates are intentionally unnamed. Fundraising is in illustrative millions of dollars, with no claim about actual receipts or disbursements.
